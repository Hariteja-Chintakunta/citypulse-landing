A broken road is easy to see. Getting it fixed is the hard part.

Hyderabad's citizens already spot the problems — they live with them every day. What is missing is a trustworthy path from "I saw it" to "it is fixed", with proof at every step.

01

Reports disappear

A complaint made by phone, paper or social media has no ticket number and no owner. The citizen cannot check it, so they assume — usually correctly — that nothing happened.

02

The same pothole, reported 40 times

Without duplicate detection, one hazard becomes dozens of separate complaints. Crews waste trips, counts inflate, and nobody knows which report is the real one.

03

No picture of the whole city

Ward officers see their own lists, but hotspots — the junction that floods every monsoon, the stretch where streetlights keep dying — stay invisible until they become news.

04

"Resolved" is a claim, not evidence

When a fix is marked done with no timeline, no remarks and no verification step, citizens stop reporting altogether. Trust, once lost, is the hardest infrastructure to rebuild.

One photo in. One tracked fix out.

The core journey from the CityPulse AI blueprint — six steps, designed so a citizen never has to wonder what happened to their report.

STEP 1

Report with a photo

Snap the issue where you stand. Location and ward are captured with the report — no forms full of questions, no office visit.

STEP 2

AI suggests, you confirm

Vision AI proposes a category, a confidence score and a severity estimate from the photo. Low confidence? It goes to a human — and you can always correct the category yourself.

STEP 3

Duplicate check

Same category, nearby, recent? The system flags a possible match before your report lands. You choose: keep it separate, or merge it into the existing ticket and strengthen it.

STEP 4

On the city map

Your issue becomes a pin on a public Leaflet + OpenStreetMap view of Hyderabad, filterable by category and ward — so neighbours can see it, back it, and watch it.

STEP 5

Admin review

A reviewer verifies the report, assigns it, and updates its status with remarks. Rejections and duplicate merges always carry a reason — nothing vanishes silently.

STEP 6

Timeline to resolution

Every status change lands on your ticket's public timeline. When it is resolved, you see when, and by whom — and you earn points for a report that led to a fix.

The ticket workflow — no black boxes

SUBMITTED→ UNDER REVIEW→ VERIFIED→ IN PROGRESS→ RESOLVED · REJECTED — with reason DUPLICATE — merged, with reason

A report is unverified until a human reviews it. AI assists the journey; it never declares an issue real, and it never closes a ticket.

Why a leaderboard?

Complaint systems usually only publish failures. CityPulse AI publishes performance: a ward that resolves quickly climbs the board, and a ward that stalls is visible to the very citizens it serves.

Combined with the hotspot analytics in the admin dashboard, the board turns individual complaints into a management tool — which streets, which categories, which wards need the next crew and the next budget line.

Try it above: submit a report in the demo and resolve it as admin — that is exactly the action that moves a ward up this board in the full app.

Quality — 20 scenarios, TC01–TC20

  • Playwright automation — the demo narrative is scripted end to end: photo report → AI suggestion → duplicate flag → ticket → map pin → admin verify → status timeline.
  • Postman API collection — report creation, duplicate matching, status transitions and rejection/merge reasons, each with expected responses.
  • Edge cases by design — low-confidence AI (human review), duplicate within metres and minutes, invalid photo, status skipping, and merges without a reason are all tested, not hoped away.
  • Led by a QA Lead — the build is owned by a QA Lead + Product Owner, so testability shaped the workflow from day one, not after it.

What CityPulse AI does not claim

A civic tool earns trust by being precise about its limits. These are designed in, not footnotes.

No government affiliation

CityPulse AI is an independent buildathon project. It is not a GHMC or government service, and no status shown here is an official municipal status.

AI suggests — humans verify

The AI proposes a category and confidence. Reports stay unverified until a human review, and low-confidence cases are always routed to a person.

Demo data is labelled demo data

Every complaint, ward figure, alert and point total in the demo is sample data, marked as such — nothing here is presented as a real, live civic record.

Privacy in photos

Faces and vehicle number plates in report photos are blurred before anything is shown publicly. A pothole needs evidence, not someone's identity.
